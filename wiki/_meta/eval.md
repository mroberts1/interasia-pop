# Inter-Asia Pop — Foundry Evaluation

Stable questions that should be answerable from this wiki. Re-run on every `/foundry-eval`. Thin/missing findings feed back into `wiki/_meta/index.md` as Open Questions and Candidates.

See `wiki/_meta/eval-usage.md` for how this document is actually used.

**Ratings:**
- `strong` — wiki has the concepts and they cite ≥2 sources (ideally mixed tiers)
- `thin` — wiki has the concepts but sourcing is weak or single-source
- `missing` — wiki can't answer

Questions are grouped by what they probe. Don't reorder or renumber — that breaks comparability across runs. Add new questions at the bottom.

---

## Definitional / framework (does the wiki know what it's about?)

**Q1. What does "Inter-Asia" mean as an analytical frame, and how does it differ from "East Asia," "Asia-Pacific," or national-cinema-style approaches?**
Probes: the vault's ability to articulate its own scope. Expected early rating: thin.

**Q2. What is techno-Orientalism, and how have Asian practitioners strategically deployed it (self-orientalisation) versus had it imposed from outside?**
Probes: concept + application, a live theoretical thread. Expected early rating: thin (YMO page exists, theory page exists).

**Q3. What does "transnational circulation" mean concretely for Asian pop media, and how is it different from "cultural export" or "soft power" framings?**
Probes: the vault's ability to hold nearby-but-distinct concepts apart. Expected early rating: thin.

## Industries / labor (does the wiki understand how the work gets made?)

**Q4. How does Japanese creative-labor structure (lifetime employment, editor-mangaka relationships, shukatsu recruitment) differ from Western precarity/freelance models, and what does each produce that the other can't?**
Probes: industry depth; should already rate strong given the Shueisha/manga-editor content. Expected early rating: strong.

**Q5. What are the structural differences between the Japanese, South Korean, Hong Kong, Taiwanese, and mainland Chinese pop-media industries, and how have those differences shaped what travels across borders?**
Probes: regional coverage. Expected early rating: missing (mostly Japan-focused so far).

**Q6. How do fan practices (piracy, subbing/dubbing, fansubs, VPN workarounds, TikTok edits) interact with official distribution channels in Asian pop circulation?**
Probes: bottom-up circulation, not just top-down industries. Expected early rating: missing.

## Aesthetic / genre (does the wiki go deeper than surface?)

**Q7. What is Shibuya-kei as an aesthetic project, who were its key figures, and what makes its "transnational soundscape" distinct from ordinary genre hybridity?**
Probes: depth on a single movement. Expected early rating: strong (Pizzicato Five, Konishi, Nomiya, Readymade already present).

**Q8. What are the aesthetic and industrial differences between City Pop, J-pop, Shibuya-kei, Wamono, and Enka, and how did each relate to Japan's economic mood at the time of its emergence?**
Probes: aesthetic taxonomy across a family of Japanese genres. Expected early rating: thin.

**Q9. What distinguishes the K-pop idol system from the J-pop idol system, structurally and aesthetically, and how did the two systems influence each other?**
Probes: comparative aesthetic-industrial analysis across two regions. Expected early rating: missing.

## Contested / critical (does the wiki hold open the disputes?)

**Q10. Where do scholars and practitioners genuinely disagree about the cultural politics of Asian pop's global rise — soft power as state success vs. capitalist commodification vs. grassroots fan achievement — and what's the strongest argument on each side?**
Probes: conflict handling, `Contested:` concepts. Expected early rating: missing.

**Q11. What has the streaming era (Netflix, Disney+, iQIYI, Viki, etc.) changed, broken, or exposed in the traditional circulation patterns of Asian pop media — and which voices argue it's democratising vs. flattening?**
Probes: present-tense debate. Expected early rating: missing.
